Questions

Can I keep my phone numbers? +

Yes. We port your existing local and toll free numbers and handle the paperwork with your current carrier.

What is the difference between a SIP trunk and a user plan? +

A SIP trunk connects a phone system you already own. Essentials and Complete replace that system with hosted phones and apps, priced per user.

Is support really 24/7? +

Yes. Phone problems do not wait for business hours, so a real person is available around the clock, including weekends and holidays.

Can I mix plans? +

Yes. Put recording only on the people who need it, and keep everyone else on Essentials. One account, one invoice.

Do you offer Business SMS? +

Yes. Text from your business number on any plan. We register your messaging campaign with the carriers so texts are delivered.
