This notice explains what information may be handled when you visit the CTX Technology website, contact us, or continue to a hosted payment page.

Effective September 29, 2026

Scope

This notice covers information connected to this public website and inquiries sent to CTX Technology. Information we handle while delivering services to a customer is governed by the applicable customer agreement and any separate data processing terms.

Information you provide

This website does not have a contact form or account registration. If you choose an email link, your email application opens and you decide what to send. CTX receives the address and message details you include, which we use to respond, discuss a request, and follow up about relevant services.

Please do not send passwords, payment card details, or other sensitive information in an ordinary email.

Information handled by the website host

When a page loads, the hosting and infrastructure providers that deliver and protect the website may process standard connection details, such as an IP address, browser and device information, the page requested, the time of the request, and the page that referred you. These details may be used for delivery, security, troubleshooting, and reliability.

Cookies and analytics

The CTX website code currently does not use analytics or advertising cookies. A third-party service you choose to visit, including Stripe checkout, may use cookies or similar technologies under its own privacy notice and settings.

Payments and other services

If you continue to a payment link, Stripe hosts the checkout page and processes the information you enter there under Stripe's Privacy Policy. Payment card details are entered with Stripe, not into this website. Stripe may provide CTX with transaction and contact details needed to confirm your order and provide the related service.

Email providers and website hosting providers may also process information when you contact us or visit the site. Their own privacy notices describe their practices.

How information is used and shared

CTX uses inquiry and transaction information to answer requests, discuss and deliver services, maintain business records, protect the website and our systems, and meet legal obligations. We may share relevant information with service providers who help us operate, with professional advisers, or when disclosure is required by law or needed to protect rights and safety.

CTX does not sell personal information collected through this website.

Retention and security

We keep information for as long as needed for the purposes described above, including to maintain appropriate business records and meet legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards for information under our control, but no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your choices and requests

You can choose not to email us or continue to a payment page. You may contact us to ask about, correct, or request deletion of information we hold about you. We will review requests under applicable law; some information may need to be retained for legitimate business or legal reasons.

Children's privacy

This website is intended for businesses and is not directed to children under 13. CTX does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through this website.

Changes and contact

We may update this notice when our website or information practices change. The effective date above shows when it was last revised. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to hello@ctx-technology.com.
